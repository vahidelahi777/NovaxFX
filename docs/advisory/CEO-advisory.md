# 👔 CEO advisory — strategy & business

Decisions synthesized from [[sources-library]] (SaaS pricing research, Tunguz,
comparable platforms) applied to NovaxFX. Feeds [[decision-log]].

## Position on the moat
Sell **validated** signals, not hype. Freqtrade's Hyperopt (grid-search the best
of 10k backtests) is the industry's overfitting trap — our deflated-Sharpe +
lockbox gate is the opposite, and it's the story. **Lead go-to-market with a
public, honest track-record page** ([[phase-0-task-board|B4 /track]] → [[system-capability-map|Epic W]]).

## Pricing (act on this — it's under-invested everywhere)
Research: startups spend ~6 hours *ever* on pricing, yet a **1% pricing gain ≈
12.7% profit**. So treat pricing as a first-class project.
- **Tiered subscription** (Free / Premium / Pro) maps cleanly to how signals
  deliver value → confirm [[phase-0-task-board|F1 tiers]]. Free = delayed signals
  + basic learn; Premium = real-time + analysis; Pro = broker hooks.
- **Talk to the first ~20 Telegram users directly**, ~daily, about value & price.
- **Roll price changes out gradually** (small cohort → 15% → 50% → full), watching
  cancellations/downgrades as the signal.

## Go-to-market
- **Telegram-first is validated** — Freqtrade proves bot-via-Telegram is a proven
  control surface. Low friction, low CAC.
- **Education as acquisition** — `/learn` content + a track-record page lowers CAC
  and builds trust; it's also the compliance-friendly framing.

## Posture / risk (protect the license-light lane)
Education + signals + bots-on-user's-own-account. No custody, no pooled funds, no
profit promises ([[decision-log|D-005]]). **Legal review before charging money or
enabling live broker execution.**

## Sequencing (revenue before risk)
Ship the **monetizable bot first** (signals + tiers + payments), *then* auto-trading.
Revenue now; the high-risk auto-execution ([[system-capability-map|Epic X]]) waits
behind the risk engine ([[decision-log|D-001]]) — never the reverse.

## Metrics to run the company on
North star: **paying subscribers**. Watch: activation (onboarded + first signal
received), free→premium conversion, churn, and signal track-record integrity.
